
# *Simon Says*
## A minecraft pack created by  the lack of restraint only attainable by sheer chaotic autism

Have you ever played minecraft normally and suddenly tought.. This game is a little too easy?
However, do you lack the necessary self control and discipline to stick with the self-made rules.

**Welcome to Simon Say's**, The minecraft resourcepack from Hell (created by one who wants to raze hell)

With Simon Says, you can spice up you're next minecraft playtrough by gaining the following:

    1. Regret.
    2. Pain.
    4. Annoyance.
    3. A constant nagging of why you are doing This.
    5. The hilarious joy of knowing that it was your own decision to play this way.

Simon Says prides itself on always keeping yourself on your toes, through constant confusion,
always ensuring joy and hapiness through our genious 'overhaul' of the crafting 'experience', and a excellent building experience by 'suggesting' the use of rarely used building blocks.
